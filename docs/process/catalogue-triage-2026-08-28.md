# The thirty-four survivors of 2026-08-28, and what they were

The full catalogue sweep at `c265a94` judged 516 of 559 entries caught
and flagged 43. Re-run one at a time on an idle machine those came
back 0 caught, 34 survived, 9 unjudgeable; the nine were ambiguous
anchors and were narrowed the same night. Judged against `v0.24.3`,
thirty-three of the thirty-four survived there too, so the catalogue
had been losing entries quietly for a version while its headline count
went on describing 559 guarded behaviours. Exactly one was this
branch's doing, and it was mended before this round began.

This is the record of deciding the thirty-four: what they turned out
to be, what settled each, and what the round could not answer.

## The finding, in one sentence

**They were not weak tests. They were facts that gained a second
writer.** Nearly every ruling of the past fortnight added a store or a
mechanism -- the group's working-state record, `_painted_ladders`
attribution asking what the plugin itself painted, a gate that refuses
a tick from a closed dialog, a follow that brings a row up to a
layer's count before the colour handlers run -- and each one left an
older single-site entry unable to fail. Nobody weakened a test. The
ground moved under the entries.

## The five kinds, with counts

**A fact with a second writer (12).** Breaking the entry's site alone
changes nothing because another mechanism answers first. Settled by
breaking both and running the CONTROL -- the other mechanism alone --
to show the co-broken half was not sufficient by itself.

**One limb of a fallback chain (3).** `group = lookup() or
findGroup(name)`, `lost = pending or watched`: either limb serves, so
an entry over one is permanently red. Re-anchored on the whole
decision, which cannot be split again by the next alternative somebody
adds.

**One fact read twice within a few lines (2).** The class-source
record seeds a combo's default and is read again to re-select it
twenty-two lines below; the run signature carries a layer's identity
and its fingerprint in one tuple. Re-aimed at the reading that
decides, found by breaking each alone.

**A journey the test does not take (11).** The commonest repair, and
the only kind that produced better tests. A Generate that changes no
geometry is a RESTYLE (`GEN-GATE restyled-instead`), so a test
asserting "and then Generate" may never reach a landing at all; a
Classify on a constant column now makes the element DEFER, so the
count guard beneath is unreachable; a reopened dialog has already
adopted its region, so `setLayer(region)` selects what is already
selected and stages no choice; a test that drives the categorized half
of a pair says nothing about the graduated twin.

**Genuinely unreachable (6).** Accepted or retired with the reason and
the condition that would reopen it: a tool the suite never runs, an
extra pass over a ladder nothing adopts, a guard whose two possible
answers coincide because `pin_problem` does not refuse on class count.

## What the repairs left behind

Five tests are materially stronger, and each closes a case the suite
had never staged: a dock reclassification the dialog cannot reconcile
must say NOTHING (it announced a ramp and cleared the element's
colours); a forward ramp set on a reversed row must be adopted as
picks rather than filed as our own seeding; a discarded pick must
leave the GRADUATED stamp as well as the categorical one; a legend
notice must reach BOTH elements sharing a column, which needed the
judgement taken out from behind an `if` and both counts staged; and
`_extreme_magnitude_layer` gained a MIXED-MAGNITUDE column, the case
the precision machinery's own docstring names and no fixture in the
suite had ever contained.

Nine entries were re-aimed or re-anchored and prove `caught`. Twelve
were retired with their measurement and the redundancy written at the
test, so somebody weakening either half meets the note. Four were
accepted. The catalogue went from 559 entries to 548.

## Three faults in my own instruments, all caught by a uniform result

Worth recording because the tell was identical each time: **a verdict
that comes back the same for every input is almost always the
instrument.**

The per-test coverage record is keyed by each test's DISPLAY name
while a catalogue entry names the FUNCTION, so the first reachability
pass answered "not in the record" for all thirty-four.
`mutate_auto.display_to_function` already existed; the mapping was
borrowed rather than written twice.

Ranking candidate tests by the SIZE of their coverage record, under a
cap, asked eight irrelevant tests about a line 503 tests touch --
region outlines and legend labels put to a mutation about layer
removal -- and "0 of 8 notice" read exactly like a finding. Ordering
cannot change a verdict when every candidate runs; under a cap it
decides what was asked.

Comparing the plugin's dumped decisions counted `region_7c23c` against
`region_bb6b7` as a changed decision, so every entry read DIFFERS.

**And twice I nearly retired entries on evidence that proved nothing**
-- once by breaking every entry on a test including the sibling that
already catches, once by pairing four survivors with catching siblings.
The control run is what caught both. A treatment whose control also
fails has measured nothing.

## What this round could NOT answer

**Which of five writers a test actually observes.** Two entries were
retired knowing only that their own site is not the one the test sees
(`_new_group_chosen` is cleared in five places). That is honest and it
is less than a full account.

**`mutation_check` applies exactly ONE replacement, by design.** A
fact held at two DISTANT sites therefore cannot be guarded by any
single entry: the run signature's identity and fingerprint terms are
47 lines apart, and an anchor that long breaks on any edit between
them. Whether the tool should take a list of replacements is a real
question and a change to campaign machinery, so it is recorded rather
than done.

**The per-test coverage record is now STALE.** The suite gained
assertions, legs and a fixture column during this round. Nothing in
the release path reads it, but `mutate_auto` refuses a stale record
and the next triage will want a fresh one: re-record before sampling.
