# The defects of 2026-08-26: a candidate that went red, and eight hunts aimed at its repairs

Written the day they were found, per the rule that a day passing
roughly ten defects gets a ledger and that the OWES column is the
record rather than any sentence above it.

THE DAY HAS TWO HALVES AND THEY TAUGHT DIFFERENT THINGS. The morning's
candidate failed three tests, on every platform, and NOT ONE was a
product defect: four tests were asserting a contract the maintainer
retired on 2026-08-25, an oracle was judging from before a legitimate
replacement, and a test was holding a widget across a debounce that
had been shortened hours earlier. The evening's eight hunts, aimed at
the seven fixes made that morning, found four real defects — three of
them confirmed by hunts that could not see each other.

THE DISTRIBUTION IS THE SAME ONE AGAIN, and it is now five rounds out
of five. Every defect below is in code written on 2026-08-25 or
2026-08-26, and three of the four are inside repairs made that day.
This project's own history: eleven of fourteen within hours on
08-16, four of five on 08-20, nine of eleven on 08-25.

AND THE SUITE COULD NOT HAVE FOUND ANY OF THEM. The candidate they
were found in had passed 574 tests, the visual gallery on three QGIS
versions, the colourspace comparison against a reference renderer
that had just moved by thirty upstream versions, and six CI jobs. A
gate measures whether known behaviour still holds.

## The harness faults, counted because a grid whose failures are its own is one nobody acts on

Five of mine in one evening, every one reading like a result:

- a probe with no message-box shim, which hung for fifteen minutes on
  a size question that can never be clicked;
- `estimate_tile_count` handed a `QgsVectorLayer` where it wants the
  GeoDataFrame the dialog has already built;
- two controls reached for by names they do not have (`new_group_check`
  for what is an entry in the group chooser, `n_spin` for a combo);
- and twice, a fixture that could not exhibit the case: a probe whose
  group had been stamped by its own landing moments earlier, so the
  branch under test had nothing to prove and the probe reported
  REFUTED.

The last is the one to remember, because it is the same trap this
file's own product-side lesson names, arriving inside the instrument.

## What the round says about hunting

EIGHT HUNTS, SEVEN WITH FINDINGS, FOUR DISTINCT DEFECTS. Three hunts
converged on defect 1 and two on defect 2, from directions chosen
before any of them had read the code — which is this method's
strongest signal and the reason the briefs name each hunt's siblings.

THE ONE THAT FOUND NOTHING EARNED ITS PLACE. It established that the
adaptive preview wait's widening is genuinely reachable from real
state (871 ms at 26 elements over a hundred columns, arming the timer
at its 350 ms ceiling; 232 ms arming it at 231), which was an open
question rather than a settled one — and it caught the false claim in
row 6 while doing it.

AND THE AIMING PAID AGAIN. Every defect here is in code written within
two days, and three are inside repairs made the same morning. Five
rounds, five times.
