# Hunt logs, 2026-09-01

The briefs and raw check-in logs from round six, ten hunts run against
the Topology tab's rebuild, the re-vendor and the save. They are kept
for the same reason as the 2026-08-13 set beside them: the METHOD is
the durable finding, and these are the only evidence of how it
actually went.

Each `*_brief.md` is the direction a hunt was given -- what to look
at, what its siblings covered, and what it was forbidden to
re-report, which is what stops a round returning one defect wearing
six costumes. Each `*_log.md` is that hunt's own record: one entry
per hypothesis, written before the next was chased, with the values
it actually read.

WHAT THE ROUND FOUND is in ROADMAP.md under 0.24.4 -- eight defects,
every one a wrong drawing or a lost file rather than a crash, all
closed with a registered test and a proved catalogue entry. What the
directions were worth is in `../HUNT-RECORD.md`.

FOUR OF THE TEN CARRY NO LOG, and that is a finding rather than an
omission: `deadaxes`, `repairs2`, `savefile` and `stochastic` have
their brief and nothing else, because they reported without keeping
a running record. This project's own reading of these logs is that
the number predicting a good report is how many of its own hypotheses
a hunt KILLED -- so a hunt with no log has given up the evidence that
would say whether it ruled anything out.

TWO CONVERGENCES ARE VISIBLE ACROSS THEM, and they are the round's
best argument for running hunts in parallel rather than in sequence:
three hunts reached the drag's disagreement with itself from
different directions, and two reached the ownership question our own
first write makes true. Neither was in any brief.

WHAT IS NOT HERE: the one-shot probes a hunt wrote to check its own
claims. Those stay in working notes, which is what `../README.md`
says of one-shot scripts generally -- they are reproductions of
claims the ledger already records, and publishing them would bury
the logs that are worth reading.
