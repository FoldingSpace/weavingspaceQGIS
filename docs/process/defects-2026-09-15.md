Nine defects, eight of them found by machines other than this Mac and none
by reading. The local suite had not run whole since rc21, and the runners
answered within an hour of the first push. What follows is one row per
defect: what a user or a reader would have lost, where it lived, and what
it owes. Everything here is closed unless the row says otherwise.

The candidate that carries them is `0.24.4rc23`, published from `1b0520b`
with every workflow green.

## What the round says

**THE COVERAGE LEG IS A WHOLE-SUITE RUN, AND IT FOUND FIVE OF THE NINE.**
The mutation workflow's coverage leg runs the suite on Linux three ways,
and rows 2 to 5 and 8 came from it rather than from the suite legs beside
it. A round that reads only the `tests` workflow is reading two thirds of
what CI ran.

**FOUR OF THE NINE WERE PREMISES THE ROUND'S OWN WORK HAD MADE FALSE.**
Whole weave gaps (74e821b) changed how many classes a weave has, and four
guards written before it went on asking for the old ones. Each passed on
this Mac until the day a full suite ran. T-72 already says a green subset
is not a green suite; what this adds is that a RULING which changes a
structure's SHAPE invalidates every fixture that counted it, so the
fixtures are the first place to look after one.

**AND A GUARD WITHHELD TOO WIDELY IS ITS OWN DEFECT.** Row 7's first
repair took the dual button away whenever a build was queued, including
the builds queued again for the design already drawn. Six existing dual
tests went red at once and said so. The repair that stood asks the
narrower question -- is this drawing of the design on screen -- which is
the question the defect was about.

**THE INSTRUMENT FAULTS WERE MINE, AS USUAL.** A probe read the glue map
at the wrong key and reported no glued classes anywhere; a bisecting probe
faked the shell command that `git` also goes through, so the commit sha
came back as JSON; and the guard staging row 7 held a build the press
makes on the MAIN thread, which deadlocked the test and reported HUNG
rather than caught. A stack sample named the last one in a minute.
