# Hunt logs, 2026-08-13

The raw check-in logs from the twelve bug hunts of that night, kept
because the METHOD is the durable finding and these are the only
evidence of how it actually went — including where it went badly.

Each log is a hunt's own record: one entry per hypothesis, written
before the next one was chased, saying what was tried, what the
result was with real values, and what that made it do next. The
conclusions drawn from them are in `../HUNT-RECORD.md`; the defects
are in `../../../ROADMAP.md` and the bug register.

Read them for the ratio, not the prose. The number that predicted a
good report was how many of its own hypotheses a hunt killed, and
these show that plainly: hunts that ruled things out found real
defects, and the one hunt that reported without ruling anything out
returned a claim that reproduced under no configuration tried.

Two honesty notes, preserved rather than tidied away. One hunt
records that seven of its timestamps were estimated instead of read.
Another logged entries out of order. Both were caught by checking the
times, which is why the record says to check them.
