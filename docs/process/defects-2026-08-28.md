## A correction to the morning's own work

**THE QUEUED-PRESS DEFECT WAS THE SUITE'S, NOT A USER'S.** Fixed
earlier the same day and made the reason for candidate `0.24.4rc3`,
its harm was written into CLAUDE.md, docs/TESTING.md, ROADMAP.md and
the candidate's tester notes as something a person met. Measured on
the evening of 2026-08-28, three ways: `generate_btn.setEnabled(False)`
runs before the task starts and only `_finish_run` and the zombie
recovery re-enable it; a probe driving a run at a fine spacing reads
`task=True` with the button disabled, and a real `click()` sets
nothing while a direct `_generate()` sets `_press_pending` at once;
and the test that failed under load,
`test_two_generates_with_different_families_keep_their_elements_apart`,
calls `dlg._generate()` three times, as `test_race_double_generate`
has for far longer.

The mechanism was exact and the fix stands as defence in depth -- a
deferred press and a deferred live tick are different facts. What was
wrong was the sentence around it, and the cost of it was a candidate
whose notes opened by telling testers to press a button the plugin
greys out for the whole run. rc3 is not to be published with those
notes. Found by `unreach`, which reported it as an unreachable guard
rather than as a defect, and by my own measurement afterwards.

THE LESSON, and it is a new one for this project: a mechanism and a
JOURNEY are two claims, and only one of them was measured. Ask of any
defect whether a person can drive the journey that reaches it, and
measure that as separately as the mechanism -- one probe reading a
control's `isEnabled()` settles it in five minutes.

## Open, and for the maintainer rather than for me

**A QUANTITATIVE STYLE ON A NUMERIC-TEXT COLUMN.** The settled rule is
that a quantitative style never stands on a text field, and its stated
reason is that a graduated renderer over text comes back with no ranges
and the layer paints nothing. Measured on QGIS 4.0.3 by the `spec`
hunt: that is true of WORDS and false of NUMERIC STRINGS. A String
column running "10" to "120" classifies exactly as its integer twin --
five ranges, the same bounds, twelve of twelve features symbolised --
and the plugin's own `classification_source` already coerces such
values while `distinct_numeric_count` already skips text that is not
numeric. So a person whose numbers arrived as text through a CSV join
or a GeoJSON cannot draw a choropleth from them at all, and is given
one colour per value instead: at three thousand areas, three thousand
and one categories.

Whether a `str`-typed column SHOULD be classifiable is a decision, and
it is yours. What is not a decision is the second half: between a
graduated style built in QGIS's own dock and the next Generate, the row
reads "Quant: Quantiles" while `_assignments` returns Categorized, and
that Generate destroys the dock's work. The follow ruling of 2026-08-17
says the row follows the layer wherever the plugin can name what the
layer holds, and a graduated renderer is nameable. Two settled rules,
one act, two answers.
