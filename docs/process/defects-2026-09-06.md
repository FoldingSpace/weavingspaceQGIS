## A question for the maintainer, from the specification hunt

`spec7` measured zigzag ruling 2's deadband against the spacing of
its own stops: the count's stops sit at `L / (2n)`, so from n=4 the
next stops lie 0.042 and 0.021 of the edge away while the deadband is
0.10 of it, and a drag past the deadband from n=4 or n=6 records only
2 or 8 -- 4 and 6 cannot be reached by dragging, only typed. The
ruling names a 12px glyph and the constant is a fraction of the edge.
Whether the deadband should be sized from the stops' spacing, or the
drag should step one stop at a time, is the maintainer's call; it is
recorded here rather than changed.
